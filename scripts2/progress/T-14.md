# T-14 — Reduced motion

**Issue:** #14  **Commit:** 08fd552  **Status:** shipped

## What shipped

`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` keeps the waveform and its response to sound, and removes the
decorative movement: the sibling drift becomes one step, a departed sibling is dropped rather
than faded, the hint is replaced without an animated fade, and the breathing opacity holds still.
The audio is byte-for-byte the same behaviour.

## Decisions made

- **`reducedMotion()` reads the media query live on every call** rather than caching the value or
  subscribing to `change`. That is what makes runtime toggling work with no listener and no
  reload, and it costs one property read per frame. A test flips the preference mid-session with
  `emulateMedia` and watches a sibling stop drifting.
- **The lerp becomes a factor of 1, not a bypass.** `rule.y += (target - y) * (still ? 1 : DRIFT)`
  keeps one code path, so there is no second way for a sibling to reach its position.
- **The ghost is dropped, not fast-faded.** `fading` is forced to 1, which falls into the
  existing delete branch. Again, one path.
- **The waveform's breathing is frozen at full**, not removed — `breath` becomes 1 so the stroke
  sits at its brightest rather than its dimmest.
- **The hint's `setTimeout` goes to 0 and the CSS transition is disabled** by the existing
  `@media` block. Both are needed: the timeout controls when the text swaps, the transition
  controls whether the opacity change is animated.
- **The discriminating test is the important one.** "Still drifts when the preference is not set"
  runs the same trace in an ordinary context and asserts intermediate positions *do* appear. A
  reduced-motion test that passes in both modes proves nothing.
- **Reduced-motion tests need their own `browser.newContext({ reducedMotion: 'reduce' })`,** so
  they cannot use the shared `context` fixture. That is fine here — none of them needs to see a
  window from another test — but any future multi-window reduced-motion test must open all its
  pages inside that one context or BroadcastChannel will not reach between them.

## Deliberately left

- **The gate's rise animation and the join control's transitions** were already covered by the
  `@media` block from T-02.
- **Nothing in `voice.js`, `engine.js` or `presence.js` consults the preference**, and nothing
  should. Reduced motion is not a reason to change what the piece sounds like.
