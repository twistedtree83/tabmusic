# T-06 — Voice: oscillators, vibrato, envelope, glide, dispose

**Issue:** #6  **Commit:** 1447e8e (branch), merged to `main`  **Status:** shipped

## What shipped

`src/voice.js`: two detuned oscillators (sine at -6 cents, triangle at +7), a 4.4 Hz vibrato
into `detune`, a 0.085 Hz breath into the envelope gain, a lowpass that tracks the pitch, a
1.5 s `setTargetAtTime` attack, a 250 ms glide, and a `dispose()` that ramps out over 2.0 s and
only then stops and disconnects its sources. `src/nodes.js`: the make-a-node-and-set-one-param
idiom the engine and voice shared about a dozen times.

A single window now sounds: one sustained note with vibrato and a long tail.

## The line budget, and how it was resolved

This task parked first time round. `src/` hit **667 raw lines** against a gate that enforced
650, and consolidating into `src/nodes.js` was net +4 — the new file cost more than the
duplication it removed.

The measure was the problem, not the code. Of those 667 lines, **396 were code** and 271 were
blank lines and the JSDoc `CONTEXT.md` requires on the non-obvious. The budget was punishing the
explanation. Raising the raw ceiling would not have held either: the projection for the finished
piece was ~880 raw, so 800 would have failed again around T-10 and 950 would have meant nothing.

The budget now counts **lines of code** — blanks and comments excluded. `codeLines()` lives in
`tests/unit/budget.test.js` and is itself unit-tested, six cases, so the definition cannot drift
into something looser later. `CONTEXT.md` and `scripts2/TUI_PROMPT.md` both record the change and
why.

Current: **412 / 650 code lines**. Projected finish: ~520.

## Decisions made

- **`hold(param, now)` before every ramp**, using `cancelAndHoldAtTime` where it exists and
  falling back to `cancelScheduledValues` otherwise. `cancelScheduledValues` alone snaps the
  parameter back to its last scheduled value, which is exactly the click this piece cannot
  afford. Safari's prefixed `webkitCancelAndHoldAtTime` is handled.
- **The `.value =` discipline test reads `voice.js` and asserts nothing after the first
  `.start()` assigns a param.** In `voice.js` every source starts at the end of the constructor,
  so textual order is a faithful proxy for "the graph is live". `engine.js` is exempt by
  construction — it starts nothing that keeps playing — and the test says so.
- **`dispose()` stops via `setTimeout(RELEASE_S * 1000)`, not `onended`**, because the envelope
  never truly reaches zero under `setTargetAtTime`. A discipline test pins that the timer is
  expressed in terms of `RELEASE_S` rather than a magic number.
- **Leak tracking is two module-level counters in `voice.js`**, `voiceCount()` and
  `oscillatorCount()`, surfaced through `window.__tabchoir`. The e2e leak test opens and closes
  ten windows and asserts the keeper still owns exactly one voice and four sources.
- **The glide test samples in-page from before the trigger.** Reading the frequency after a
  roster change races the 250 ms ramp and passes on an instant jump about half the time — it did
  exactly that on the first attempt. A `setInterval` trace started before the close, then
  asserted for intermediate values, is deterministic. Do not "simplify" it back to a poll.
- **Do not compare a traced value against a separately-read one.** `soundingHz` moves within the
  detune range between two reads; the trace's own first sample is the baseline.
