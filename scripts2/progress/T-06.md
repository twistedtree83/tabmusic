# T-06 — Voice: oscillators, vibrato, envelope, glide, dispose

**Issue:** #6  **Commit:** 1447e8e (branch `t-06-voice`, not merged)  **Status:** parked

## What shipped — on the branch, not on main

`src/voice.js`: two detuned oscillators (sine at −6 cents, triangle at +7), a 4.4 Hz vibrato
into `detune`, a 0.085 Hz breath into the envelope gain, a lowpass that tracks the pitch, a
1.5 s `setTargetAtTime` attack, a 250 ms glide, and a `dispose()` that ramps out over 2.0 s and
only then stops and disconnects its sources. `src/nodes.js`: the make-a-node-and-set-one-param
idiom the engine and voice shared about a dozen times.

**Every acceptance criterion on #6 is met and green.** 35 Playwright tests and 73 unit tests
pass, including the leak test and the `.value =` discipline test.

**It is not on `main`,** because `src/` is 667 lines and the verify gate enforces 650.

## Why this is parked rather than worked around

`RALPH_PROMPT.md` forbids committing a red gate, and `scripts2/TUI_PROMPT.md` says the fix for
the budget is consolidation — "not deleting tests, not inlining everything into one file to game
the count". I did the consolidation. It did not recover enough, and everything left would change
something documented rather than something written. That is a fork the bibles do not resolve, so
it goes to the human.

What I tried:

- **Consolidated the repeated node construction** into `src/nodes.js` (`gain`, `osc`, `lowpass`).
  Removed 26 lines from `voice.js` and `engine.js` and added 30 in the new file: **net +4**. It
  is a genuine readability improvement and I kept it, but it is not a budget fix.
- **Counted what the budget is actually measuring.** Of 667 lines, **396 are code** and 271 are
  blank lines and JSDoc. `CONTEXT.md` requires comments on the non-obvious — the deterministic
  roster sort, the tritone rule, why `pagehide`, why `roleOctave` filters — so the budget as
  written penalises a rule the same bible imposes.

## The decision needed

The budget is documented in two places — `CONTEXT.md` ("under **650 lines**") and the T-02
acceptance criteria — and the brief that generated them said "~600 lines. If it's ballooning,
the abstraction is wrong." Three ways out, in my order of preference:

1. **Count code lines, not raw lines.** Currently **396 / 650**. Keeps a real guard, stops the
   budget fighting the commenting rule. Weakest against future bloat, since it roughly doubles
   the effective ceiling.
2. **Raise the raw budget to ~800.** Currently **667 / 800**. Simplest, preserves the existing
   measure, and 800 raw ≈ 470 code is still a tight guard for what remains (T-07 through T-16
   add the tritone wiring, canvas, overlay, and four edge cases).
3. **Cut something from the piece.** The feedback echo is ~6 lines and came from the design file
   rather than the brief; the breath LFO is ~4. This buys the least and costs the most — both
   are things you can hear.

Reordering does not help: bringing T-08 forward deletes the ~25 lines of `<pre>` dump
scaffolding from `main.js`, which lands at 642 — eight lines of headroom, consumed by T-07.

**The abstraction is not wrong.** Nine modules, each with one job, the two that carry the
musical logic pure and heavily unit-tested. The piece simply does more than 600 lines of
commented JavaScript, and the gate is measuring the comments.

## Deliberately left

- **`avoidTritone` is still not wired in.** **T-07** owns it, and it will need ~6 lines.
- **The engine's `ready` promise is still unawaited.** A voice starts a few milliseconds dry.
  Judged not worth blocking the attack for; revisit if it is audible.
- **`dispose()` uses `setTimeout` for the stop**, not an `onended` handler, because the envelope
  never truly reaches zero under `setTargetAtTime`. The timer is `RELEASE_S * 1000` and the
  discipline test pins that relationship.
