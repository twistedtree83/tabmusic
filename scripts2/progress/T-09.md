# T-09 — Stage canvas: sibling rules, drift, and departing ghosts

**Issue:** #9  **Commit:** 648fa2d  **Status:** shipped

## What shipped

One faint bone rule per sibling, drawn across the canvas at a vertical position derived from
that voice's pitch — high pitch high on screen, log scale so an octave is always the same
distance. Positions lerp at 0.08 per frame. A departed peer becomes a **ghost**: its rule keeps
drawing for 2 s while opacity and stroke width fall to zero, then it is dropped.

Three windows open now read as a chord before you hear one.

## Decisions made

- **`renderStage(root, analyser, siblings)`** takes a getter rather than a snapshot, so the
  stage pulls the current roster each frame instead of the roster pushing at it. The call moved
  below the `roster`/`selfId` declarations in `main.js`; it must stay there.
- **Listeners are filtered out before the stage sees them.** A listener is in the roster but not
  in the chord, so it gets no rule. The filter lives in `main.js`, not `stage.js`, because
  "which voices are sounding" is a chord question.
- **Rule positions are stored as fractions of the canvas height**, not pixels, so a resize does
  not teleport every sibling.
- **The ghost map lives in `stage.js` alone.** `presence.js` has no concept of a ghost, which is
  what keeps the "a ghost counts for nothing" property true by construction rather than by
  discipline.
- **Sibling rows are detected by colour arithmetic, and the reasoning matters.** Bone at 0.22
  over ink lands near (72, 72, 70): grey, with red and blue within a few points. The amber
  waveform's antialiased edges reach similar reds but keep a blue near 34. `Math.abs(red - blue)
  < 12` is what separates them. Scanning at x = 2% of the width also puts the sample where the
  waveform's taper is narrowest.
- **That detection only sees a ghost above roughly half opacity** — below `remaining ≈ 0.45` the
  red falls under the threshold — so the visible window is about 1.1 s of the 2 s fade, not the
  full 2 s. The "still fading" assertion has a 900 ms budget for that reason, and the
  ghost-accounting test does not assert visibility at all. If a later task makes the fade longer
  or the opacity lower, that 900 ms is the number that breaks.
- **The drift test samples in-page from before the move**, the same technique T-06 needed for the
  glide. At 0.08 per frame the drift is over in a few hundred milliseconds and a poll afterwards
  sees only the endpoint.

## Deliberately left

- **No typography.** **T-10** owns the overlay.
- **The drift lerp ignores `prefers-reduced-motion`.** **T-14** owns making it instant, and
  `DRIFT` is the constant it will need.
- **Ghost fade and hint fade are both animated regardless of the preference.** Also **T-14**.
- **The rAF loop still never stops.** **T-15**.
